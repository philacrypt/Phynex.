What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember information about your visit, like items in your cart or your preferences, and can also be used to understand how the site is used.

How PHYNEX uses cookies

Category Purpose Can you turn it off?
Necessary Keeps your cart, checkout and login working properly. Without these, the Site can't function correctly. No — always on
Analytics Helps us understand how visitors browse and use PHYNEX, so we can fix issues and improve the shopping experience. Yes
Marketing Used to show more relevant offers and measure the effectiveness of campaigns, on PHYNEX and elsewhere. Yes

Managing cookies in your browser

In addition to the preferences you set on PHYNEX, most browsers let you control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting them. Note that blocking necessary cookies may stop parts of the Site, like your cart, from working correctly.
