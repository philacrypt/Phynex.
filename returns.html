Returns & Refunds

Information about returning products, requesting a refund and resolving problems with an order.

Our returns policy

If you receive an item that is damaged, defective, incorrect or otherwise does not meet the applicable return conditions, you may be able to request a return.

Return eligibility can depend on the product, seller and condition of the item.

Please keep your order information and the original packaging until you are satisfied with your purchase.

How to request a return

1

Contact PHYNEX

Contact our support team and provide your order details and the reason for the return.

2

Provide the required information

You may be asked to provide photographs or other information about the product.

3

Return the product

If your return is approved, you will receive instructions on how and where to return the item.

4

Refund processing

Once the returned product has been received and assessed, the applicable refund will be processed.

Condition of returned items

Products should generally be returned in an appropriate condition and, where applicable, with their original packaging, accessories and documentation.

  • Do not remove important product labels unnecessarily.
  • Include accessories that came with the product.
  • Keep the product safely packaged during the return.

Refunds

Refunds are processed after the return has been reviewed and approved.

The time it takes for the money to appear in your account can depend on the payment method and the financial institution processing the transaction.

Your refund amount and eligibility will depend on the circumstances of the return and the applicable PHYNEX return policy.

Items that may not qualify for return

Certain products may have restrictions on returns because of their nature, condition or applicable regulations.

Always check the product information and applicable return conditions before completing a purchase.

Received a damaged or incorrect item?

If your order arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, contact PHYNEX support as soon as possible.

Include your order number and clear information about the issue so that our support team can assist you.
