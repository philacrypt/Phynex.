Warranty

Information about product warranties, warranty claims and what to do if a product develops a fault.

Warranty information

Some products sold through PHYNEX may include a manufacturer's or seller's warranty.

Warranty coverage, duration and conditions can vary depending on the product, manufacturer and seller.

Always check the warranty information provided with the specific product before making a purchase.

What may be covered

Depending on the applicable warranty, coverage may include faults or defects that occur during normal use within the warranty period.

  • Manufacturing defects.
  • Hardware faults covered by the manufacturer.
  • Other issues specifically included in the product warranty.

What may not be covered

Warranty coverage generally does not apply to damage caused by circumstances outside the applicable warranty terms.

  • Accidental or physical damage.
  • Improper use or installation.
  • Normal wear and tear.
  • Unauthorized repairs or modifications.
  • Damage caused by misuse or neglect.

How to make a warranty claim

If you believe your product has developed a fault covered by its warranty, contact PHYNEX support.

1. Find your order information

Have your order number and product details available.

2. Describe the problem

Explain the issue and how it occurred. Photos or videos may be requested when appropriate.

3. Wait for assessment

The applicable seller or manufacturer may need to assess the product before determining the next step.

4. Follow the provided instructions

If the claim is approved, you will receive information about the available resolution.

Important information

  • Warranty periods vary between products.
  • Keep your proof of purchase and order information.
  • Do not attempt unauthorized repairs before contacting support, as this may affect warranty eligibility.
  • The final warranty decision may depend on the manufacturer's or seller's warranty terms.
