How selling works

1

Create your seller account

Sign up instantly with your business and contact information — no waiting for approval to get started.

2

Submit your products

Add product names, descriptions, prices, photographs and other relevant information from your seller dashboard.

3

PHYNEX reviews each listing

Every submitted product is checked by the PHYNEX team before it goes live on the store.

4

Receive orders and dispatch

Once approved, customers can discover your products, place orders, and you fulfil them following the applicable delivery process.

What you need to sell

Sellers may need to provide information required to verify their account and products.

  • Accurate seller and contact information.
  • Clear product descriptions and photographs.
  • Accurate product prices and availability.
  • Reliable order fulfilment.
  • Compliance with PHYNEX marketplace requirements.

Seller responsibilities

Sellers are responsible for ensuring that the products they list are accurately described and available for sale.

Sellers must also fulfil orders and follow the applicable PHYNEX policies relating to products, delivery, returns and customer service.

Seller requirements, fees and marketplace terms may change as the PHYNEX marketplace develops.
