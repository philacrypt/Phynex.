This policy is provided as a starting point for PHYNEX and is not legal advice. Have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer familiar with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 before you launch, and update the details in brackets to match how PHYNEX actually operates.

1. Who we are

PHYNEX ("PHYNEX", "we", "us", "our") operates an online marketplace at this website, allowing customers to browse, order and pay for products delivered within Kenya. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at phynex70@gmail.com or via our contact page.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly, such as when you place an order, create an account, or contact support:

  • Name, phone number and email address
  • Delivery address (county, town/city, street address, delivery instructions)
  • M-PESA phone number used to complete a payment
  • Messages you send through our contact form
  • Order history and items in your cart

We also collect some information automatically when you use the site, such as your device type, browser, approximate location, and how you interact with pages — this is covered in more detail in our Cookie Policy.

3. How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To process payments through M-PESA (Safaricom Daraja) and confirm transactions
  • To communicate with you about your order, delivery, or support requests
  • To respond to messages sent through our contact form
  • To improve the site, catalogue and overall shopping experience
  • To detect and prevent fraud or misuse of the platform

4. Who we share it with

We share the minimum information necessary with:

  • Delivery partners, so your order can be delivered to the address you provide.
  • Safaricom / M-PESA (Daraja API), to process your payment. We do not store your M-PESA PIN.
  • Service providers who help us run the website (e.g. hosting, email delivery), bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities, where required by Kenyan law or to protect PHYNEX's legal rights.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep your cart working, remember your preferences, and — where you allow it — understand how the site is used. You can manage your preferences at any time from our Cookie Policy page or the cookie settings button on any page.

6. Data retention

We keep order and account information for as long as needed to fulfil orders, meet accounting and tax obligations, resolve disputes, and comply with Kenyan law. Contact form messages are retained only as long as needed to respond to and resolve your enquiry.

7. Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we hold about you and why
  • Access a copy of your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal and legitimate business requirements
  • Object to certain uses of your data, including marketing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at phynex70@gmail.com.

8. Keeping your data safe

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
